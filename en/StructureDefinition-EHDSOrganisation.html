Logical Model: Organisation model

Official URL: http://ehds.eu/fhir/models/StructureDefinition/EHDSOrganisation Version: 0.1.0
Draft as of 2026-07-06 Computable Name: EHDSOrganisation

EHDSOrganisation means a structured set of data elements describing an organisation.

Usages:
