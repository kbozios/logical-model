Logical Model: Care plan model

Official URL: http://ehds.eu/fhir/models/StructureDefinition/EHDSCarePlan Version: 0.1.0
Draft as of 2026-06-14 Computable Name: EHDSCarePlan

EHDSCarePlan means a structured set of data elements describing the intended management of a patient's health conditions but excluding pharmacological treatment plans that are represented through medication model.

Usages:

  • Use this Logical Model: Discharge Report model, Encounter model, Imaging report model and Patient summary model

You can also check for usages in the FHIR IG Statistics
